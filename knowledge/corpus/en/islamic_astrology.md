# Ilm al-Nujum: Classical Islamic Astrology

## The Basic Framework

In the Islamic scientific tradition the study of the heavens was gathered under
*ilm al-nujum* (the science of the stars). Al-Farabi divided the field in two:
*ilm al-nujum al-ta'limi*, the observational and mathematical discipline we now
call astronomy, and *ilm ahkam al-nujum*, the interpretive discipline concerned
with the significations of the celestial bodies (*dalalat al-kawakib*) for human
character and circumstance.

The system rests on the premise that continuous motion in the supralunar sphere
produces causal change in the sublunar world of forms, which is composed of four
elements (*anasir al-arba'a*): fire, air, water, earth.

## The Abu Ma'shar Tradition

Abu Ma'shar al-Balkhi, the most influential astrologer of the ninth century,
set out in his *Kitab al-Mudkhal al-Kabir* (Great Introduction) an eclectic
synthesis describing the qualities of the signs, the characteristic natures of
the planets, and their dignities.

- **Sun**: sovereignty, honour, the father, spirit, vitality. Ruler of Leo.
- **Moon**: the mother, the public, emotion, changeability, travel. Ruler of Cancer.
- **Mercury (Utarid)**: writing, calculation, trade, eloquence, intellect.
  Gemini and Virgo.
- **Venus (Zuhra)**: love, art, beauty, pleasure, harmony. Taurus and Libra.
- **Mars (Marikh)**: conflict, courage, iron, cutting force, anger. Aries and
  Scorpio.
- **Jupiter (Mushtari)**: abundance, wisdom, justice, religion, expansion.
  Sagittarius and Pisces.
- **Saturn (Zuhal)**: time, patience, limit, old age, trial, depth. Capricorn
  and Aquarius.

## Elements and the Four Temperaments (personal chart analysis only)

IMPORTANT LIMIT: The mapping below is between the four ELEMENTS and the four
humoural temperaments — a temperament is NOT read from a single sign. A
person's temperament is measured only from the whole birth chart, from the
elemental distribution of all the planets. This mapping therefore applies
only in a personal chart analysis; asserting "this sign is of this
temperament" in a GENERAL horoscope (without individual birth data) is a
cliché and is contrary to the classical tradition itself.

- **Dominant Fire**: choleric — hot and dry; bold, initiating, pioneering.
- **Dominant Earth**: melancholic — cold and dry; patient, deep, practical.
- **Dominant Air**: sanguine — hot and moist; sociable, quick-minded,
  communicative.
- **Dominant Water**: phlegmatic — cold and moist; intuitive, feeling,
  private.

## Al-Biruni and Nativity Analysis

Al-Biruni's *Kitab al-Tafhim* establishes the logic of reading a birth chart:
the configuration of the sky at the moment of birth is a map of temperament and
life themes. The ascendant (*talih*) shows the body and outward bearing; the Sun
shows spirit and honour; the Moon shows emotional nature and one's relation to
the public. Planets placed in houses colour the domains of life — property,
siblings, parents, children, illness, marriage, death, travel, vocation,
friends, adversaries.

## Principles of Interpretation (For Practice)

1. A single indicator is never sufficient; at least three are combined before a
   judgement is offered.
2. Interpretation maps tendencies, it does not pronounce fate: "the stars
   incline, they do not compel."
3. Difficult configurations are named plainly and read as pressure points —
   what the period demands, not what it forbids.
4. The balance of temperament — how the four elements are distributed — is the
   ground of every reading.
