Frequently asked

What's in the free tier?

Daily/weekly/monthly sign readings, the live sky screen, 5 chat messages a day, one I Ching cast a day, the friends list and streak tracking — free forever.

How does Rytho+ work?

A monthly subscription with the personal daily reading and a monthly allowance of 300 tokens. Deep reports cost tokens (chat 1, I Ching 2, natal/BaZi 5). Token packs can be bought separately and never expire.

Where does my data live?

Your birth data is stored encrypted on Google Cloud and used only to produce your readings. Face reading is measured on your device — your photo is never uploaded. Details in the privacy policy.

Is this a fortune-telling app?

Rytho is an entertainment and insight app. It meets ancient traditions with real astronomical computation; it gives no medical, legal or financial advice and makes no claims of certain futures.
